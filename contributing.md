# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this list more useful. Quality and honesty matter more than length.

## Inclusion criteria

A tool or resource qualifies if it is:

1. **Real and reachable** — the link works and the project exists.
2. **Free to use** — at minimum a meaningful free tier; prefer fully free / open source.
3. **Privacy-respecting** — it does not require uploading your resume to an opaque third party. Client-side or self-hostable tools are preferred.
4. **Relevant** — it helps someone get a resume past an ATS (checking, parsing, formatting, or applying).

## How to submit

1. Fork this repository.
2. Add your entry to the correct section of `readme.md` in the format:
   `- [Name](https://link) - One-sentence description ending with a period.`
3. Open a pull request. In the PR description, include a **one-line justification**: why it fits the criteria above.
4. If you maintain or are affiliated with the tool, **disclose it** in the PR.

## Style rules

- Keep descriptions factual. No "best", "#1", or "definitive" claims.
- Descriptions start with a capital letter and end with a period.
- One entry per line. Keep alphabetical-ish ordering within a section where it reads naturally; featured/criteria-defining entries may lead a section with a brief note.
- Don't add a tool just because it's yours — add it because it's genuinely useful.

PRs that don't meet the criteria, or that read as pure self-promotion, will be declined.
